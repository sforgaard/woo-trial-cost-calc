💡 Strategic Context

🔓
Gateway Freedom
WooCommerce has no gateway surcharges. Merchants can use Stripe direct (with IC+ pricing at scale) or any processor - potentially achieving lower rates than either WCPay or Shopify Payments. Shopify penalizes non-SP gateways with a 0.15%-2% surcharge.
🎁
Growth Engine Product Value
The GE doesn't compete on payment rates - it provides $2K-$5K+/yr in free products (AutomateWoo, Subscriptions, Metorik, Pressable hosting, Jetpack Security). Switching to Shopify means buying equivalent products.
🔒
Compounding Lock-In
Each GE unlock creates a dependency: data history, workflow automation, subscription models, hosting infrastructure. Even if Shopify is cheaper on paper, the migration cost and payback period make switching economically irrational for most established stores.
